WHY JOIN & WHO CAN APPLY

We welcome all IISER Bhopal students who are passionate about technology, regardless of their department or prior programming experience.

Key Member Benefits:

  • Access to hands-on hardware lab benches, microcontrollers, and compute
  • Opportunity to work on real-world engineering and research projects
  • Mentorship from senior students and EECS faculty advisors
  • Team formation & funding support for national hackathons
  • Networking with industry professionals and research alumni
  • Certificate of active participation for club contributors

Open Domains & Roles:

[⚡ AI & Deep Learning] [🛠️ Embedded & Robotics] [🌐 Web & Systems] [🎪 Event Logistics & Ops] [🎨 Media & Creative Design]
HOW TO JOIN & ELIGIBILITY

How to Join:

Recruitment happens through our annual Trainee Induction cycle. When applications open, submit your technical interest form, complete a hands-on beginner assignment, and attend a friendly interview discussion with club coordinators.

Eligible Academic Streams:

[✓] Engineering Sciences (EECS, DSE, ChemE)
[✓] BS-MS Natural Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Math, Bio, Earth)
[✓] Economic Sciences & All Academic Years (1st to 5th Year)

Next Application Window:

Autumn 2026 Induction timeline will be announced at Club Orientation.
